Tracy (Huiwen) Han

I am a Research Assistant in the Brain Stimulation Lab at Stanford University, working with Dr. Christopher Austelle and Dr. David Spiegel, and a visiting student in the PHAI Lab at Texas A&M University, advised by Dr. Zhiwen Fan. I received my B.A. in Biology from Boston University, with minors in Computer Science and Business Administration.

I am curious about how machines learn, reason, and keep evolving over time. My interests include generative models, neurosymbolic AI, and continual learning. So far, I have built diffusion models for compositional 3D brain MRI generation, graph learning models that classify depression from neuroimaging, and reinforcement learning methods for long-horizon LLM agents. At Stanford, I built the multimodal clinical-trial data infrastructure behind our psychiatric neuromodulation trials, funded by a Stanford Rigor & Reproducibility grant.

Science for fun and for hope!
